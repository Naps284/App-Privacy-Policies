Privacy Policy

Simple Blackjack Offline
com.naps284.simpleblackjack

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In brief: The app works offline. It does not collect or share personal data through its own code. Game progress and settings are saved locally; Android may back them up or transfer them depending on your device settings.

1. Developer and scope

This policy explains how Simple Blackjack Offline (the “app”), developed by Nathan Milesi (Google Play developer name: NathanNaps284), Switzerland, handles user information. You can contact the developer about privacy at naps284.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data collection, use and sharing

The app is a single-player offline blackjack game. It does not require an account, registration or an internet connection. It does not request personal information or access contacts, location, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers or payment information.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, integrated crash-reporting services or app-operated servers. Neither the app nor its bundled libraries are designed to transmit user information to the developer or third parties. Virtual game credits have no monetary value and are not real-money transactions.

3. Local storage and security

The app uses Android Jetpack DataStore to save game progress, virtual credits, gameplay statistics, unlocked and selected tiers, last bet, game rules and preferences such as animation speed, haptic feedback and strategy highlighting. This information is used only to provide gameplay features and preserve progress and settings.

These records are saved in the app's private internal storage, protected by Android's application isolation and applicable device-level security features. The app does not add its own encryption to these files. The developer does not have remote access to them.

4. Android backup and device transfer

Android's system-managed backup and restore features are enabled for the app. Depending on your Android version, device, manufacturer and backup settings, the operating system may include the saved game data and preferences in cloud backups to your Google account or in transfers to another device. The app itself does not operate a cloud synchronization service, and the developer does not receive or control these backups.

You can manage backup options through your device or Google account settings. See Android backup and restore information and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local game information is retained on your device until it is overwritten or removed. The in-app Reset Progress option resets game progress and statistics but retains gameplay settings. You can remove all local app data using Android Settings > Apps > Simple Blackjack Offline > Storage > Clear storage (names vary by device), or by uninstalling the app. Existing system backups may be retained or restored according to Android's backup settings; manage them separately through your device or backup account. There are no app accounts or developer-held gameplay records to delete remotely.

6. Google Play and Android services

Google Play and Android may independently process information related to installation, updates, device operation and diagnostics, depending on your settings and their own policies. These platform activities are distinct from the app's own data handling. See Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Support communications and privacy rights

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, your email address and the contents of your message will be processed to respond to your inquiry, provide support or handle a privacy request. This processing is separate from the app. Messages are handled using Gmail (Google); Google's privacy policy describes its processing, including possible processing outside Switzerland. The developer will retain correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes or as required by law, and will not use it for marketing. Where applicable, processing is based on the legitimate interest in responding to inquiries or complying with legal obligations.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal information held by the developer, or exercise other applicable privacy rights, by using the contact address below. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority, including the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. As the developer does not receive your local gameplay records, their deletion is managed on your device as described above.

8. Children's privacy

The app does not ask for or collect personal information from users of any age through its gameplay. It is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please contact the developer.

9. Privacy policy website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This site does not include developer-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or cookies. GitHub's handling of visitor information is described in its General Privacy Statement. Visiting this website is separate from using the offline app.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date. If the app's data practices change, its privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.
